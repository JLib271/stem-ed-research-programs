STEM+ Education Research Graduate Programs

Graduate programs and research groups in STEM education research, including discipline-based education research (DBER) and medical and health professions education. Open and community-maintained.

Programs and research groups are listed whether or not they are taking students right now. Ask the faculty about openings.

Add or update a program

The list lives on GitHub. Use the add or update form there, or email the maintainer if you don't have a GitHub account. Other DBER communities keep their own lists. The GitHub page links to them.
