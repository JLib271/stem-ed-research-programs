STEM+ Education Research Graduate Programs

Graduate programs and research groups in STEM education research, including discipline-based education research (DBER), medical and health professions education, the history and philosophy of science, human dimensions, and science and health communication. Open and community-maintained.

Programs and research groups are listed whether or not they are taking students right now. Ask the faculty about openings.

    Suggest or update a program

    Know a graduate program or research group in STEM+ education research that isn't here? Send these five details:

    1. Institution and country
    2. Home department, the unit that grants the degree
    3. Degrees, for example MS and PhD
    4. Discipline, from the list above
    5. Education research faculty, people who agree to be listed

    Email the details Use the GitHub form

    The email button opens a message with the five questions filled in. The GitHub form needs a free GitHub account.

    Update a listing

    Every program card has an Update this listing link. Use it to:

    • correct a detail, such as a department or degree
    • confirm the listing is still correct
    • add a faculty member, or remove yourself
    • remove a program that no longer exists (programs not taking students stay listed)

    It offers the same two choices: an email that already contains the current listing, so you only edit what's wrong, or the GitHub update form.

    Everything is reviewed before it appears.
