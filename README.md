# STEM+ Education Research Graduate Programs

An open, community-maintained list of graduate programs and research groups in
**STEM+ education research**: research on teaching and learning in science, technology,
engineering, mathematics, medicine, and the health professions. It includes:

* **Discipline-based education research (DBER)** in science, engineering, and mathematics
  departments
* **Science, mathematics, and STEM education** programs in schools and colleges of education
* **Medical and health professions education** research in medical, nursing, pharmacy,
  and other health professions schools
* **History and philosophy of science** (including science and technology studies and the
  history of medicine) and **science and health communication**, as fields in their own
  right as well as in teaching

These programs sit in many different kinds of departments, so they are hard to find by
searching for "STEM education." This list brings them together in one place.

It lists programs and research groups whether or not they are taking students right now.
Being listed does not mean a group has openings, so ask the faculty directly.

Each program records five things:

| Field | What it records |
|---|---|
| **Institution** | University or college, and country |
| **Home department** | The department, school, or institute that grants the degree, and any partner units |
| **Degree types** | BS, MA, MS, MEd, MAT, MHPE, EdD, PhD, or Certificate |
| **Discipline** | Agriculture, Bioscience, Chemistry, Earth Science, Engineering, Health Education (including medical and health professions education), History & Philosophy of Science, Learning Science, Mathematics, Physics, Science & Health Communication, or Science Education. These are the STEMEd+ disciplines shared with [STEMEd+ Jobs](https://github.com/JLib271/stemed-jobs), Funding, and the Digest, kept in [HuMeta](https://github.com/JLib271/HuMeta) (`standard/humeta/stemedplus_disciplines.json`). |
| **Education research faculty** | Faculty who supervise education research students |

🔎 **Search and filter the list:** https://jlib271.github.io/stem-ed-research-programs/

## Add or update a program

* **On the website:** use **Suggest a program**, or the **Update this listing** link on any
  program card. Both offer a prefilled email (no GitHub account needed) or the GitHub form.
  The update email already contains the current listing, so you only edit what's wrong.
* **No GitHub account?** Email the maintainer (see below) with the five fields.
* **With a GitHub account:** use the [Add a program](../../issues/new?template=add_program.yml)
  or [Update a program](../../issues/new?template=update_program.yml) form, or open a
  pull request that adds or edits a file in [`programs/`](programs/).

Faculty are listed only with their agreement. If you are listed and want to be removed
or corrected, open an issue or email the maintainer. See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) for details.

## Data and license

Each program is one YAML file in [`programs/`](programs/), checked against
[`schema/program.schema.json`](schema/program.schema.json). Each file also records
where its information came from (`source`) and when someone last confirmed it
(`last_verified`). Fields that still need confirming are listed in `needs_check` and marked ⚠️ above.
